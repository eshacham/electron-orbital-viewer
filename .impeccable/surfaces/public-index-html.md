## Direction contract

THESIS: A precise brass scientific instrument: the specimen is lit in a dark viewing field and the chrome is matte
metal and engraved type. It refuses the generic blue SaaS panel and every literal laboratory costume (no gears,
rivets, parchment or gold gradients).

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian #141513 / panels #22231F with ivory ink, or warm ivory #F4F0E6 / #FFFCF5 with umber ink; one
matte brass (#C4A15A) or bronze (#806020) accent for action and selection; selection is a brass-tinted fill plus a
brass edge, never colour alone. Source Serif 4 for the wordmark, the specimen title and Info's title; Inter with
tabular figures for every control and number. Hairlines, 4–8 px corners, no glows.

STORY: The learner sees one lit specimen with its name engraved above it; the researcher finds every method and
limit in quiet ivory type. Basics feels warmer, brighter and roomier; Research is cooler graphite, pewter brass and
dense.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Unchanged structure: top bar (serif QuantumLab in brass, the mode tabs, the level row, search),
Explore left, the dark viewing field centre with the serif specimen title top-left and the toolbar top-right, the
strip along the bottom, the inspector right. In the light theme the viewing field stays dark.

FORM: Pinned by the owner's brief (concept pair, instrument dark/light), no roll; signature move: the engraved
graticule, a fine brass scale bar with graduations and warm axis marks framing the specimen. seed: none (pinned) — no roll by design: the owner pinned the direction on 2026-11-01 ("prototype brass, replace both themes if I like it"), choosing the concept pair docs/quantumlab/references/brass/quantumlab-instrument-{dark,light}.png

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
