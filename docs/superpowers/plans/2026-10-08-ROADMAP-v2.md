# Roadmap v2: useful for research, product development and teaching

**Supersedes** the remaining phases of `2026-09-25-ROADMAP.md` (Phases 1–6
and 6B-1…6B-3 are shipped; see `docs/HANDOFF.md`).
**Decided:** 2026-10-08, approved by the owner. Scientific and product calls
are the developer's, made from the research below; business calls are the
owner's.
**Evidence:** `docs/research/2026-10-08-user-needs-and-gaps.md` (what
researchers, scientists and teachers need, and what this app lacks),
`docs/research/2026-10-08-scans.md` (what a scan should be, methods, costs,
ceilings), `docs/research/2026-10-08-business-model.md` (pricing and
business model).

**Goal:** the free tool researchers, product developers and teachers reach
for to see and compute electronic structure — scientifically credible first,
then the features they ask for most, then a paid compute service that pays
for itself.

## Order

Each phase ships on its own (tests green, reviewed, deployed). Phases 2–4
share the job code and run one after another; phase 5 can run alongside.

1. **Phase 6C — quotes and approval** *(in progress)*. Every on-demand job
   shows Spot and on-demand options with estimate and maximum (compute,
   storage, delivery, platform-fee lines); the owner approves one; the
   approved maximum sets the job's time limit and caps the charge. Fixed
   1 h / 2 h ceilings become a sanity limit only. Large molecules get a
   quote, plus a labelled cheaper-basis option (e.g. C₆₀ at def2-SVP).
2. **Phase 6D — scientific credibility** (~1 day).
   - Molecules mode: orbital labels in the molecule's full point group (not
     PySCF's D2h subgroup — methane's HOMO is t₂); honest handling of
     diffuse-basis "LUMO" artefacts; "not the optical or fundamental gap"
     caveat; honest wording where validation is circular (experimental
     geometries).
   - On-demand jobs: B3LYP-D3(BJ) dispersion; a frequency check after every
     optimisation (true minimum, no imaginary modes); molden/QCSchema output.
   - Worker bugs found by the scans research: `symmetry=True` blocks
     constrained motion; density-fitted gradients crash with the locked
     NumPy (pin or upgrade); add `pyscf-dispersion` to the image.
   - Worker time budget: stop optimisation in time to run the final single
     point on the last geometry, marked "not converged", instead of timing
     out.
3. **Phase 6E — vibrations and IR** (~1–2 days). Frequencies, IR spectrum
   and mode animation for the library and on-demand jobs; Bonds mode gains
   ωe, ZPE, D₀ and vibrational levels from its existing curves.
4. **Phase 6B-4 — scans** (~2–3 days), per `docs/research/2026-10-08-scans.md`:
   1-D bond/angle/dihedral scans, relaxed by default (rigid optional);
   defaults 24 × 15° dihedral, 13 angle, 20 bond points, ≤ 36; geometries at
   B3LYP-D3(BJ)/def2-SVP with def2-TZVP single points when within the quote;
   broken-symmetry UKS with ⟨S²⟩ for bond breaking; the worker's own
   point-by-point loop (warm start), split into ≤ 4 chains as a Batch array
   job; outputs: profile in kcal/mol and kJ/mol, minima and barriers,
   multi-frame XYZ, Walsh diagram; UI: atom picker, snapping/animating
   slider, "open this point as a molecule", priced by the quote.
   v2 later: 2-D scans, NEB/IRC, CCSD(T) single points (≤ ~4 heavy atoms).
5. **Phase 6F — open your own files** (~1 day, parallel). Load molden (then
   fchk/cube) in the browser and view orbitals/surfaces with the app's
   renderer — no desktop licence needed for figures.
6. **Phase 7 — teaching and research presence** (existing plan
   `2026-09-25-phase-7-learning-and-research.md`): guided lessons, methods
   page, citation/DOI, embedding. Needs the owner's domain, licence,
   analytics and Zenodo decisions.
7. **Phase 8 — public launch and payments.** Business model per
   `docs/research/2026-10-08-business-model.md`: free viewer, library,
   lessons and already-computed molecules; prepaid USD balance through a
   Merchant of Record (Paddle recommended) at a markup over AWS cost
   (4× recommended), $10/$25/$50 top-ups, failed or interrupted jobs never
   charged; private results as a researcher option; free tier only after
   abuse limits exist. Must exist before charging: domain with terms,
   privacy and refund pages; public sign-up with SES email; per-user
   balances (hold the quote's maximum, settle actual); payment webhooks for
   refunds and chargebacks; per-user limits; the AWS safety budget rescaled
   to revenue. Owner decisions: model and markup, Paddle vs Stripe Managed
   Payments, domain, an accountant on payout taxes.

**Later:** charges (Hirshfeld/CM5, ESP-fit) and bond orders, localised
orbitals; polyatomic MO energy-level diagrams and simulated photoelectron
spectra; method/basis comparison; UV-Vis by TD-DFT; lab balances and
classroom licences.
